## Project Overview

This project focuses on the simulation and study of the electrical characteristics of PN junction and Zener diodes using Tinkercad Circuits. The project examines diode behavior under forward and reverse bias conditions and studies the voltage-regulation property of a Zener diode.

## Objectives

- To study the forward-bias characteristics of a PN junction diode.
- To study the reverse-bias characteristics of a PN junction diode.
- To investigate the forward-bias characteristics of a Zener diode.
- To study the reverse breakdown characteristics of a Zener diode.
- To understand the operation of a Zener diode as a voltage regulator.
- To record observations, plot characteristic graphs, and compare diode behavior.

## Results and Observations

- The PN junction diode conducts significantly in forward bias after reaching its forward-conduction region.
- The PN junction diode offers high resistance under reverse bias before breakdown.
- The Zener diode exhibits forward-bias behavior similar to a conventional PN junction diode under the tested simulation conditions.
- Under reverse bias, the Zener diode enters its breakdown region and maintains an approximately constant voltage over the operating range studied.
- The Zener shunt regulator demonstrates the use of a Zener diode for voltage regulation.
